# MASTER WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
## Premium Industrial Metals Sourcing & Trading Platform

### ROLE AND OBJECTIVE

Act as a senior product designer, UX architect, B2B industrial-commerce specialist, full-stack engineer, cybersecurity engineer and business owner.

Design and build a complete, production-oriented website and operational enquiry platform for **[COMPANY NAME]**, an India-based industrial metals sourcing and trading business.

Think like the founder of this business. Every design decision must help establish credibility, generate qualified enquiries, simplify sourcing operations, protect confidential commercial information and support long-term business growth.

This must not be a generic website template or a traditional e-commerce store.

The website must be modern, visually premium, responsive, technically sound, commercially practical and designed for buyers, manufacturers, scrap suppliers, recyclers, procurement teams and international trade enquiries.

Do not create fake content to make the business appear larger or more established than it is.

---

# PART 1 — BUSINESS MODEL

The business initially focuses on sourcing and supplying:

- Refined copper and copper products
- Copper scrap
- Aluminium and aluminium scrap
- Steel and steel scrap
- Brass, zinc, lead and other metals as operations expand

The company initially operates using a **requirement-led sourcing model**:

1. A buyer submits material specifications, quantity and delivery requirements.
2. The company reviews the enquiry.
3. Suitable suppliers and material options are identified.
4. Specifications, availability, inspection requirements and commercial terms are checked.
5. A quotation is prepared and sent to the buyer.
6. Upon agreement, order documentation, payment terms and delivery arrangements are confirmed.
7. Dispatch and delivery are coordinated according to the agreed transaction structure.

The company does not initially operate its own warehouse. Do not claim that it owns factories, trucks, warehouses, laboratories or material stock.

Direct supplier-to-buyer delivery may be arranged where commercially and operationally appropriate.

The website must distinguish between a **sourcing enquiry**, a **verified available offer** and a **confirmed order**.

The platform should initially serve Indian customers and support international enquiries, without claiming established export operations or international offices.
